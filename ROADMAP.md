# Telescan Public Roadmap

This is product direction, not a release promise.

## Current MVP

- Apple/Telegram account linking and per-device sessions
- BLE discovery with local distance hints
- Device-local Met and Saved profiles
- Profile, BIO and photo management
- Reports, blocks and account deletion
- Production monitoring and encrypted database backups

## Before public launch

- Apple Developer enrollment and production signing
- TestFlight and App Store validation
- Accessibility and supported-iOS regression testing
- Monitoring calibration and recurring recovery drills

Later work may include improved accessibility, localization, private BLE
identifiers and broader moderation tools. Android remains deferred until the
BLE contract is stable.
